That file is the schematic view of the Fritzing core part
`capacitor_elko_goldcap_4.0F_92eef0d1d2f548b9db9918a1769107d3_2`
("Electrolytic Capacitor" / Goldcap), whose author is Christoph Wempe; its own
`<desc><referenceFile>` names `capacitor_polar.svg`, i.e. Fritzing's built-in
**polarized** capacitor symbol (used here because a supercapacitor is
polarized, unlike the ceramic capacitor symbol already used by
svg/_assets/ceramic_capacitor_schematic.svg).
